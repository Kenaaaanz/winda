Tenant quick start

  1. Open Find Properties to browse available homes and review each listing's details.
  2. Submit an application from a property listing, then follow its status under Applications.
  3. Review lease documents under My Leases and complete any requested signing steps.
  4. Use Payments to review rent and other payment records. Check the due date and amount before paying.
  5. Use Messages to contact the property owner and Notifications to see account updates.
{% elif role == 'HOUSE_OWNER' %}

Property owner quick start

  1. Open My Properties to manage listings, or choose List Property to add one. Keep property and unit details up to date.
  2. Review tenant requests under Pending Applications, then manage accepted tenants and lease documents from the tenant and lease sections.
  3. Use Payments to review transactions and Analytics to monitor property activity.
  4. Review maintenance requests and coordinate with tenants or caretakers through Messages.
  5. Complete your profile and payout/bank setup to receive property payments.
{% elif role == 'PROPERTY_SCOUT' %}

Property scout quick start

  1. Use Submit Listing to send a property for review. Follow its verification status from your dashboard.
  2. Your dashboard shows listing activity and recent commissions. A commission is recorded when an eligible platform fee is collected.
  3. Complete your payout account details in Profile before requesting an available payout.
  4. Review payout progress and payment status in the payout section of your profile. A transfer may remain processing until confirmed.
  5. Use Notifications for verification and payout updates.
{% elif role == 'CARETAKER' %}

Caretaker quick start

  1. Open your dashboard to review assigned properties, units, and the owner responsible for them.
  2. Use Maintenance to follow requests for your assigned properties and check their current status.
  3. Use Messages to contact the owner or tenants and Notifications to review updates.
  4. Your available actions depend on the permission level set by the property owner. Contact the owner if you need access changed.
